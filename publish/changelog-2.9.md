## I.M.T Technology 2.9: the firearms update (Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.251+)

The big update: four new firearms, an attachment system, and enemies who carry them.

**New firearms** (real 3D models; built from six parts at the Gunsmith Table, like the Glock 19)
- **MP5**: a 9mm submachine gun with a 30-round magazine and a fire selector (safe, semi, burst, auto).
- **AK-47**: an assault rifle in 7.62x39mm (safe, semi, auto). Hits harder, kicks harder.
- **AWP**: a bolt-action sniper rifle in .338 Lapua Magnum. Press R after every shot to work the bolt.
- **Remington 870**: a pump shotgun. Eight pellets per shot, a tube you load shell by shell, pump with R.
- New ammunition for each caliber, pressed at the Reloading Bench. Long guns hang on a Gun Sling.
- X turns the fire selector. Every new gun has a safety.

**Attachments** (made and mounted at the Gunsmith Table, tab "Make parts")
- **Red Dot Sight** for every firearm, and a **Sniper Scope** that zooms from 1x to 10x (scroll while aiming).
  You look through the scope: no black overlay.
- **Suppressors** per caliber: quieter for guards and players, no muzzle flash, a little less damage.
- **Vertical Grip**, **Angled Grip**, a **Bipod** for the AWP (sneak and stand still) and a **Tube Extension**
  for the Remington 870.
- **Extended magazines** for the Glock 19, MP5, AK-47 and AWP.
- The Red Dot Sight, the Sniper Scope and the grips also go on the laser guns, at the Laser Workbench.

**Raven soldiers**
- Soldiers now carry the MP5, AK-47, AWP and Remington 870 too. Each fights differently: bursts, a sniper who
  aims first and shoots from far away, a shotgunner who closes in.
- What is mounted on a soldier's gun stays on it when he drops it. Their backpacks hold ammo for their own gun.
- Attachments and ammunition turn up in the chests of the bunker and the tower.

**Also**
- "How it works" (hold W on an item) explains the new guns and the attachments.
- New settings under `[guns]` and `[raven]` in `config/imttech.toml`.

Install it on both the client and the server.
